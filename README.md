# Kraft Academy

**Power Yourself for the Future**

Kraft Academy is an education technology website designed to help school students learn practical AI and coding skills in a structured, accessible, and outcome-oriented way. The platform focuses on students from Grades 6 to 12, with programs built around academic support, future skills, coding fundamentals, AI productivity, and responsible usage of AI tools.

The website acts as the primary digital touchpoint for parents and students. It introduces the academy, explains the value of AI and coding education, showcases the course structure, communicates trust, and guides users toward booking a demo class or enrolling in a program.

Live Website: https://kraftacademy.vercel.app/

## About the Project

Kraft Academy is a student-focused learning platform created to bridge the gap between traditional school education and emerging AI-driven skills. As AI tools become increasingly common in academics, productivity, research, creativity, and career preparation, students need structured guidance on how to use them responsibly and effectively.

The website was created as a landing page and conversion funnel for Kraft Academy. It helps parents understand why AI and coding skills matter, what the academy teaches, how the programs are structured, and how students can begin with a demo session.

The project is not just a static website. It is designed as an early-stage digital product that supports discovery, trust-building, lead generation, demo booking, and eventual enrollment.

---

## Problem Statement

Most students today are exposed to AI tools, but they often lack proper guidance on how to use them productively. Parents are also unsure whether AI should be treated as a distraction, a shortcut, or a serious academic advantage.

Common problems this website tries to address include:

- Students use AI tools without understanding how to ask better questions.
- Parents want their children to learn future-ready skills but do not know where to start.
- Schools often teach computer subjects in a theoretical way, while students need practical exposure.
- Coding and AI are often taught as separate technical topics, instead of being connected to real academic use cases.
- Students need a safe, structured environment where they can learn AI without misusing it.

Kraft Academy solves this by positioning AI as a study companion, productivity tool, creativity enabler, and future skill.

## Purpose of the Website

The website has four main purposes:

## Target Audience

The website is built for two primary user groups.

### Parents

Parents are the main decision-makers. They want to know whether the program is useful, safe, practical, affordable, and relevant to their child's future.

The website addresses parent concerns such as:

- Will this help my child academically?
- Is AI safe for students?
- Is this only about coding, or does it also help with school studies?
- What will my child learn?
- How much time will the course require?
- Is there a demo before enrollment?

### Students

Students are the end users of the program. They are interested in tools, creativity, projects, shortcuts, exam help, and interactive learning.

The website speaks to students by showing that AI can help with:

- Homework support
- Notes and summaries
- Quiz generation
- Exam preparation
- Creative projects
- Coding confidence
- Better learning habits

## Key Features

### 1. Clear Hero Section

The hero section introduces Kraft Academy with a strong headline, short explanation, and call-to-action. It is designed to quickly communicate the benefit of the platform and guide users toward booking a demo.

### 2. Demo Booking Focus

The website is structured around a demo-first funnel. Instead of forcing users to enroll immediately, it allows parents and students to experience the teaching approach before committing.

### 3. Program Overview

The site explains the available learning programs, including AI future skills and coding-focused tracks for school students.

### 4. Academic Use Cases

The website connects AI learning with school outcomes. It shows how students can use AI for notes, summaries, quizzes, homework assistance, study planning, and exam preparation.

### 5. Parent-Friendly Communication

The copy is written in a way that helps parents understand the value of AI without making the platform feel overly technical.

### 6. Small Batch Positioning

The platform emphasizes focused learning through smaller batches, which improves student attention, personalization, and doubt-solving.

### 7. Mobile-Friendly Layout

The website is designed to work across devices, especially mobile phones, because parents and students often discover educational programs through social media, WhatsApp, and mobile browsing.

### 8. Strong Calls to Action

Multiple call-to-action sections are placed across the page to reduce friction and help interested users take the next step.

## User Journey

The website follows a simple product funnel.

### Step 1: Discovery

Users discover the website through social media, referrals, campaigns, WhatsApp, or direct sharing.

They land on the homepage and quickly understand what Kraft Academy offers.

They evaluate the structure, target grades, course relevance, and demo option.

They click on a call-to-action such as "Book Free Demo" or "Enroll & Get Discount".

The user enters the demo or enrollment flow, which can later be connected with payments, WhatsApp confirmations, or CRM workflows.

## Product Thinking Behind the Website

The website was designed using a product-first approach rather than just a visual-first approach.

Key product decisions include:

### 1. Demo-First Conversion

A demo class reduces hesitation for parents. It allows them to experience the quality of teaching before paying for a full program.

### 2. Parent-Centric Messaging

Since parents are the main buyers, the website explains outcomes in terms of academics, confidence, discipline, and future readiness.

### 3. Simple Funnel

The website avoids unnecessary complexity. The journey is intentionally simple:

Landing page → Program understanding → Demo booking → Enrollment

### 4. Trust Over Hype

Instead of presenting AI as a magic shortcut, the website positions it as a responsible learning tool.

### 5. Clear Academic Relevance

The platform links AI usage to real academic tasks such as revision, quizzes, summaries, homework, and study planning.
